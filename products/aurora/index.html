AURORA Iridescent Reflective Everyday Running Cap

An everyday running cap with a solid crown, micro-mesh sides, an iridescent reflective-film logo and a purple reflective-film arrow. Built for commutes and easy runs.

Size for AURORA

AURORA is the ENDÛRE cap for every day: commutes, easy runs, and the days between the long ones.

The crown is solid woven fabric with no perforations. The side panels are an engineered micro-mesh knit and the rear panel is open-knit mesh, so the cap breathes through mesh rather than laser-cut holes.

The ENDÛRE NEW YORK front logo is cut from iridescent reflective heat-transfer film, with a purple reflective-film arrow over the U.

It has a 2.75 in (7.0 cm) brim that folds flat and springs back to shape, a dark matte brim underside, and a dual-face knit sweatband around the full circumference. A 3 mm elastic shock cord and a silicone toggle adjust the fit with one hand. Size M fits heads 54–57 cm; size L fits 57–60 cm. The target weight for size M is 42 g or less.

Materials: front panel, crown panels and brim 92% polyester, 8% elastane; side and rear mesh 100% polyester; sweatband 85% polyester, 15% elastane.

Care: machine wash cold (30 °C max) on a gentle cycle and air dry. Don’t bleach, dry-clean or iron the logo. A cold rinse after each run clears sweat and salt.

Designed in New York. Made in China.
